## ADR-005: Captcha → human solves it, crawl pauses (2026-10-03)
- Status: accepted (explicit user request)
- Context: spike I1 hit «Доступ ограничен: проверка безопасности» (HTTP 439) on warm-up; user solved the captcha manually in the bot's browser window (`python -m app.auth`) and asked for this flow.
- Decision: bot never solves/bypasses captcha. On captcha/block page during /report: send Telegram alert «🧩 Avito просит капчу — пройди её в окне браузера бота», pause the crawl (browser stays open, HEADLESS=false), poll the page every ~30 s for up to CAPTCHA_WAIT_MINUTES (default 30); when the block page is gone → continue; on timeout → stop as before (save state, send collected finds). Supersedes the "stop immediately on block" part of AC-5.x for /report. Monitor (D&G) behaviour unchanged (alert + skip).
- Plan impact: implement in I11 (block handling); requirements AC-5.1 to be updated accordingly.
- Implemented (I11): `MarketCrawler._fetch` catches `ProviderBlocked`, sends the alert (with the wait in minutes), and calls `provider.wait_unblocked(url, CAPTCHA_WAIT_MINUTES*60, poll 5 s)`: a separate tab on the blocked URL is polled until `is_blocked` is false; the load is then retried once (counts as a load). Timeout, `/stop` during the wait, `HEADLESS=true` or `CAPTCHA_WAIT_MINUTES=0` -> no wait; status `blocked`, finds kept, summary sent, resume via /report within 12 h. Requires `HEADLESS=false`. The browser (and the BrowserGate) stays held during the wait, so a monitor scan waits too (worst case CAPTCHA_WAIT_MINUTES). Monitor behaviour unchanged (alert + skip).

  10. LOW: batched `_lines`; `_finish` wrapped (the summary is sent even if the DB write fails); `CrawlRun.note` removed from the model (the column stays in old DBs); the progress message is sent once and not retried every minute on failure; Moscow time (`msk_now`, zoneinfo + tzdata on Windows) for relative dates and the crawler clock; «29 февраля» resolves to the previous leap year; `link_preview_options` instead of `disable_web_page_preview`.
  11. Section display names: static `SECTION_NAMES` (25 slugs) in `market_logic`.
  12. Docs: AC-5.1 refers to ADR-005, AC-5.3 states the captcha-wait exception, AC-2.4a/4.2/4.4 updated, tech-design cleaned up (section 12).

## ADR-008: Roadmap batch F1-F5 (2026-10-03)
- Status: accepted (orchestrator; the user delegated decisions)
- F1 goofish link: `goofish_query(title)` keeps Latin tokens (letters/digits/-/.; at least one letter; length >= 2), drops generic words (new, original, orig, size, cm, mm, kg, set, lot), max 4 tokens in order, None without a model. `format_find` appends `🔎 goofish` (urlencoded + escaped). Tokens without a Latin letter (e.g. «15» in «iPhone 15») are dropped by design.
- F2 `/top [days]`: DB only (no Avito loads), dedup by `group_key` keeping the best vpd, sorted by vpd, max 15, `split_message`.
- F3 `/export`: CSV (utf-8-sig, `;`) as a document; cells starting with `= + - @` get a leading `'` (formula injection in Excel).
- F4 `Category.skipped` (idempotent `ALTER`, generalized `_ADDED_COLUMNS` in `init_db`). `/cats`, `/skip text`, `/unskip text` match slug, Russian section name or subcategory name case-insensitively in Python (SQLite `lower` does not handle Cyrillic), minimum 2 chars. `crawl_order` and `MarketCrawler._categories` exclude skipped categories (progress totals too).
- F5 feedback: simplest variant. A portion with <= 8 finds gets numbered lines and an inline keyboard, one row per find (`👍 n`/`👎 n`, callback `fb:<find_id>:<1|-1>`); the callback sets `Find.feedback` (overwrite, not accumulate). Callbacks are filtered to the admin chat. `crawl_order(..., feedback)` multiplies priority by `feedback_factor(net)` = clamp(1 + 0.5 * net, 0.25, 2.5), net = sum of feedback over the category's finds. Portions with more than 8 finds and the final summary have no buttons. `send_text` got an optional `markup` argument (only passed when a keyboard exists).

## ADR-009: goofish query fix, model aggregation, margin (2026-10-03)
- Status: accepted (orchestrator; the user delegated decisions)
- G1: `goofish_query` keeps number tokens that sit right next to an accepted Latin token («iPhone 15 Pro Max», «DDJ 400»); 4-digit years (19xx/20xx) are dropped; max 4 tokens. Nothing is added to the title: the earlier «Apple iPhone Pro Max» came from my own test title that already contained «Apple», not from brand injection; the number «15» was dropped because of the old "needs a Latin letter" rule, now fixed.
- G2: `model_key(title)` = first 2 Latin tokens (+ numbers between/directly after them), lowercased; None unless the key has a digit or 2 Latin words. All 4 real night titles («Aimiko u2 pro premium 3000w» etc.) give `aimiko u2`: version words (pro/premium/power) are not part of the key because they are variants of one model whose spread is shown as vpd and price ranges. Known limit: «Nike Air Max» and «Nike Air Force» both give `nike air`; the block is informational only. `model_groups` counts distinct `external_id` and keeps models seen >= 2 times, sorted by count x max vpd; shown in the final summary (whole run) and in `/top` (whole N-day window, before dedup by group).
- G3: settings `CNY_RATE=12.2`, `CARGO_RUB_PER_KG=500`, `CARGO_AIR_RUB_PER_KG=3000`; weight presets `WEIGHT_PRESETS` (by regex over title + category name, first match wins, default 3 kg). Each find line shows `#<find_id>`; `/price <id> <yuan>` stores `Find.china_price` and replies with cost (yuan x rate + kg x ground rate), sale price (the find's cheapest price), margin and its percent of sale, plus the air margin. `/top` adds a margin line under finds with a known China price.

## ADR-010: Recheck of past finds, «ушло за N дней» (2026-10-03)
- Status: accepted (orchestrator; the user delegated decisions). MARKERS NEED LIVE CONFIRMATION.
- Decision: at the start of `/report`, before discovery and crawling, `MarketCrawler._recheck_finds` opens up to `RECHECK_MAX` (default 20, 0 = off) finds that are 2-14 days old and not gone yet, oldest-checked first, one open per `external_id` (a listing found in several runs is opened once, all its rows are updated). Opens go through `_fetch`/`_before_load`, so they count against the run budget and follow the ADR-005 block path; the loop stops early when the budget is reached. Other errors skip the find.
- New columns (idempotent ALTER): `finds.gone_at`, `finds.last_checked_at`, `finds.views_last` (views at the last recheck; `views` keeps the original value).
- Gone detection (`avito_parser.is_gone`): HTTP 404/410 (`Page.status`, new field), or no views counter and a `TEXT_PATTERNS["gone"]` marker in the title/first 3000 chars of visible text («Объявление снято с публикации», «Объявление закрыто», «Товар продан», «Объявление больше не доступно», «Страница не найдена»). «Товар зарезервирован» is not gone (fixtures `market_item*.html` verified). There is no live fixture of a removed page: the marker words are from memory of Avito's UI and must be confirmed on a real removed listing; a missed marker just means the find is rechecked again later.
- Output: the summary gets «✅ Ушло: N (за ~X дн)» with title link, days to gone, vpd and price; `format_find` adds «✅ ушло за X дн» (so `/top` shows it); the model block shows «✅ ушло K» for models with gone listings. Gone finds stay in the DB and in the model aggregation.

## ADR-015: Market history foundation (Radar V2, Phase 0–1) (2026-10-03)
- Status: accepted (user: «начинай»). Design: `docs/radar-v2-design.md`, research: `docs/deep-research.md`.
- Phase 0 check — **item id is not a creation-time watermark**: on 35 finds with a real date (seller/page) the Spearman correlation of id and date is 0.02 (Avito sorts and dates by last activation/raise). The «id watermark» incremental stop from the design is dropped; the sweep (Phase 2) stops by the share of already-known non-promo ids on a fully read page plus the 7-day and depth limits.
- History = one row per listing + change events, not a snapshot per sighting. New tables (created by `create_all`, no ALTER): `ads` (id = avito item id, category, title, `model_key`, price, `url_path` without query, city, shop name when the search card has it, seller url from the card, image url, best known `posted_at` with source search < card < seller, `first_seen_at`/`last_seen_at`, `promoted_seen`, `status` live|gone), `ad_events` (price/title/status changes), `card_obs` (views/today per card open; bucket report|recheck|backfill), `scan_categories` (per run × category: pages, non-promo cards seen, new vs known, `total_count`, covered hours, stop reason). `crawl_runs` plays the role of the design's `scan` table.
- `/report` behaviour is unchanged; it now records: every search page right after it is read (own commit, so a block or stop mid-category keeps the pages), every opened card (`card_obs`, date and seller url refine the ad), the seller-profile date, recheck views (`recheck`) and gone status (event). Status «gone» still relies on unverified markers (ADR-010) — it is not «sold».
- New parser field `parse_total_count` (`page-title/count`, «17 888»): free supply series per category, read from page 1.
- Fix: an empty first search page while the header says N > 0 listings is a layout failure, not «the whole week covered» (covered = 0, stop reason `empty`). An empty page without a counter keeps the old meaning.
- `scripts/backfill_history.py` moves existing finds into `ads` + `card_obs` (idempotent via bucket `backfill`). Run on 2026-10-03: 34 listings; DB backup `data/app.db.bak-20261003-phase1`.

## ADR-016: Daily incremental sweep of all categories (Radar V2, Phase 2) (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user: «дальше идем»).
- Avito «Аналитика спроса» is **not** used: it needs a paid tariff (~8 000 ₽). Demand stays our own measurement (card views, baseline per category — Phase 4).
- `/sweep` and a daily job read only search pages of every non-skipped category — no cards, no seller profiles. A run is `crawl_runs.kind = 'sweep'` with its own budget `SWEEP_BUDGET` (800); `/report` runs are `kind = 'report'` and keep `REPORT_BUDGET`. A run resumes only a run of the same kind (12 h window, as before).
- Depth per category: `rate` = median of `cards_seen / window_hours` over its last 7 finished scans (any kind; windows < 1 h ignored); `pages = ceil(rate × hours since last scan / 50 × 1.3) + 1`, clamped to [2, `SWEEP_MAX_PAGES`=10]; no history → `SWEEP_FIRST_PAGES`=3. Categories with < `SWEEP_QUIET_PER_DAY` (25) new listings/day scanned less than 36 h ago are skipped (every other day). Order: longest since last scan first.
- Stops (same `_collect` as `/report`): 7-day age limit, empty page, depth cap, and for the sweep `known` — a fully read page where ≥ `SWEEP_KNOWN_STOP` (0.85) of non-promo listings were already in `ads`. No id watermark (ADR-015).
- Checkpoint: after every page `scan_categories` gets a `done = false` row (`stop_reason = partial`) in the same commit as the listings; a resumed sweep continues that category from the next page with the counters kept. `/report` still restarts an interrupted subcategory from page 1.
- Schedule: APScheduler cron `DAILY_SWEEP_AT` (09:00 Europe/Moscow, misfire grace 6 h, max_instances 1) plus a one-off catch-up 2 min after bot start; both call `MarketCrawler.daily()`, which starts/resumes a sweep only if the time has passed today, nothing is running and today has no `done`/`budget` sweep (a `blocked`/`stopped` one is resumed). `DAILY_SWEEP_AT=""` disables the schedule.
- Telemetry: progress message «📡 Обход рынка» (new listings instead of finds); final message with categories done / total, quiet skipped, pages, loads, new vs known listings, stop reasons, captcha waits, errors, and «не досмотрено» for depth-capped categories with covered hours.
- Data: the 209 regular categories were un-skipped (brand mode is over); all 257 categories are active. `/skip` turns any back off.

## ADR-017: MARKET RADAR v1 from search history (Radar V2, Phase 3) (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user: «что дальше?» → next phase).
- The sweep's final message is now the radar (one message; split only if > 4096 visible chars): `📡 MARKET RADAR · DD.MM`, coverage (categories scanned today / active, new listings today, history days), sections, and the sweep telemetry (ADR-016 summary) in an expandable quote. `/radar` sends the same radar from the DB without loads and without telemetry.
- No `category_day` table: aggregates are computed on the fly from `scan_categories` (30 days) and `ads` (17 days); fine at our volume, materialize only if it gets slow.
- Category velocity (new listings/day): scans of a day are merged (new summed); with a previous day's scan and a full stop (known / age_limit / empty) = new × 24 / hours since that scan, coverage 1; otherwise (first scan, depth cap) density = cards seen × 24 / covered hours, coverage = covered / elapsed hours. Scans < 1 h apart give no estimate. Search dates are day-granular, so the density estimate is biased low — consistent across days, used only as relative.
- Trend (needs ≥ 3 earlier days within 14): base = median velocity; growing if today ≥ 10/day, ≥ 1.5× base and ≥ base + 3√base; cooling if base ≥ 10, ≤ 0.6× base and ≤ base − 3√base; else stable; before that «unknown» and the report says trends appear after 3 days. Shelf stock: today's `total_count` vs the earliest within 7 days.
- Confidence = min(volume/20, history days/14, coverage), ×0.8 if the same state was not there yesterday; HIGH ≥ 0.7, MEDIUM ≥ 0.4, LOW otherwise. MEDIUM+ goes to «🔥 Предложение растёт» / «🧊 Остывает», LOW only to the collapsed «👀 Наблюдаю».
- Models (`ads.model_key`, date = `posted_at` or first seen, city = first segment of `url_path`): «🔁 Модели у многих продавцов · 7 дн» — ≥ 3 listings in ≥ 2 cities, ranked by cities then count, with shops, median price, main category, link to an Avito search by the model; «🚀 Новые модели» — ≥ 5 listings in ≥ 3 cities in the last 3 days and ≤ 2 in the 14 days before, shown only after ≥ 7 days of history.
- Not yet (Phase 4+): attention (views vs category baseline), «Исследовать сегодня», persisted `signal` table.

## ADR-018: Survive IP blocks without a human; steady sweep pace (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user: bot must run full time; on 2026-10-03/04 Avito answered HTTP 439 «Вы робот?» with no captcha for hours, the run ended `blocked` and the browser closed).
- Still no bypass (ADR-005): no captcha solving, no fingerprint spoofing, no IP rotation.
- `_fetch`: on a block the human wait runs first (unchanged); if nobody passes it (or there is nothing to pass), the crawler pauses `BLOCK_COOLDOWN_MINUTES × 2^n` (60 → 120 → 240) up to `BLOCK_COOLDOWNS` (3) times, telling Telegram «⏸ … продолжу сам в HH:MM», with the browser left open and `/stop` honoured, then retries the same page. Only after the pauses run out does the run end `blocked`. Pauses are counted in the sweep summary.
- `wait_unblocked` counts as passed only when the probe tab holds a real Avito page (`data-marker=`); a blank tab used to count as passed (false «✅ Проверка пройдена»).
- Sweep pace: extra `SWEEP_PAUSE_SECONDS` (10 s, ±50%) before each sweep load on top of the provider delay: a slow, even rate instead of bursts. `/report` keeps its pace.
- Researched alternatives (not done): a data provider for search results ([rest-app.net](https://rest-app.net/api), 4 000 ₽/month, all categories, no view counts) would replace the sweep with zero blocks, leaving only card views to the browser; a single static RU residential/mobile proxy (datacenter IPs burn within an hour per [kalinkindev 2026](https://kalinkindev.ru/blog/kak-parsit-avito-2026/)). User decision pending.

## ADR-019: One Avito traffic controller; sample huge categories (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user: «сделай так, чтобы не было этой проблемы» — the «Доступ ограничен: проблема с IP» page). Research: `docs/avito-rate-limit-research.md`, design: `docs/avito-traffic-v2.md`. Automatic captcha solving (2captcha etc.) was requested and declined: it is circumvention of Avito's protection (ADR-005).
- `app/providers/traffic.py` `AvitoTraffic`, shared by MarketCrawler and Scanner (built in `main.py`): before every page load `acquire(kind)` waits for `AVITO_MIN_INTERVAL_S` (45 s, ±50%; ×`SLOW_FACTOR` in slow mode), keeps ≤ `AVITO_MAX_PER_HOUR` (60) in a sliding hour, and refuses beyond `AVITO_DAILY_BUDGET` (450 page loads/day across search/card/profile/recheck/monitor) or after `MAX_BLOCKS_PER_DAY` (3) blocks. Day counters live in table `avito_traffic` (day × kind), so a restart does not reset them. It only slows down; never faster than the base interval.
- Block handling in `MarketCrawler._fetch`: every block is counted; the 3rd block of the day ends the run `blocked` («продолжу завтра»). Human passed the check → `POST_CAPTCHA_COOLDOWN_MIN` (5) without loads, then slow mode (`SLOW_HOURS` 2). No human → pause 60/120 min (`BLOCK_COOLDOWNS` now 2), then slow mode. `SWEEP_PAUSE_SECONDS` removed (the controller owns pace).
- The block page stays open in its tab; `wait_unblocked` watches that tab's DOM (no reload, no new navigation) — the human presses «Продолжить» and solves the check there.
- Huge categories: the sweep reads only page 1 when the search header shows ≥ `SWEEP_LARGE_TOTAL` (20 000) listings or the category's history rate is > `SWEEP_LARGE_PER_DAY` (150/day) — stop reason `sample` (radar uses density, coverage < 1). Normal categories are capped at `SWEEP_MAX_PAGES` = 4.
- Scanner no longer pre-empts a market run: if the browser is held, the monitoring tick is skipped (old test `test_scan_waits_gate` removed). Its loads go through the same controller (`monitor`).
- Expected volume ≈ 450 loads/day instead of 1 500–2 500; a full sweep takes several hours in the background. Telemetry line «Загрузки сегодня: N / 450 (search …, card …) · блоков · капч · медленный режим до HH:MM» in the sweep summary.

## ADR-020: Crawl scope — CORE / WATCH / QUERY / EXPLORE / OFF / HARD_EXCLUDE (Product Hunter, phase 1) (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user approved `docs/product-hunter-blueprint.md` with corrections). Goal of the whole track: every morning 5–10 concrete models to check in China; categories only feed that.
- `app/scope.py` is the source of truth, keyed by category path (+ query) — stable across installs, unlike DB ids. All 209 map categories are listed explicitly: CORE 25 (`scope_status` = hypothesis until data confirms), WATCH 18, EXPLORE 54, OFF 101 (0 daily requests, manual review; includes «Игровые приставки и аксессуары» marked `duplicate_of` the other console category), HARD_EXCLUDE 11 (regulated: supplements, medical devices, sports nutrition, jewellery hallmarking, cosmetics ×4; incompatible: stamps, documents, coins). Of the 48 brand × gender slices 16 are QUERY_WATCH, 32 OFF; plus 2 new queries `DJI Mini`, `DJI Avata` (`EXTRA_QUERIES`). Flags: LOGISTICS_CHECK / REGULATORY_CHECK / FAKE_RISK. The earlier «208» came from the duplicate id 102 being in no list; name matching also failed on non-breaking spaces — hence the coverage test.
- Invariant: tests check 209 real keys, exact counts per scope, single `duplicate_of`, query counts, and — when `data/catalog.csv` is present — that the scope keys equal the map keys. `apply_scope` runs at bot start (and in `scripts/check_scope.py`): sets `kind/scope/flags/duplicate_of`, creates missing extra queries, marks categories missing from `scope.py` as OFF with a warning (newly discovered or imported subcategories start OFF too).
- Crawl: sweep visits CORE daily (depth as ADR-016/019), WATCH and QUERY one page every ≥ 44 h, EXPLORE one page every ≥ 10 days and at most 6 per run; OFF/HARD_EXCLUDE never. `/report` uses CORE/WATCH/QUERY only. Expected search pages/day ≈ 60 (CORE 35 + WATCH 9.8 + QUERY 9.8 + EXPLORE 5.4), `expected_search_loads`.
- Dedup: a listing first seen in a query slice keeps `ads.query_id`; its `category_id` moves to the real category when the listing shows up there, and it counts as new for that category; seen again in the slice it stays «known». Product signals will count distinct `external_id`.

## ADR-022: Product identity — normalization, brand/model extraction, products, offline rebuild (Product Hunter, phase 2) (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user command «Phase 2»). Principle: a false merge is worse than a false split; unsure → LOW/UNKNOWN, no key.
- `app/services/products.py` (`PRODUCT_EXTRACTOR_VERSION = 1`), local and deterministic, no LLM/embeddings/network: `normalize` (casefold, ё, diacritics, quotes/dashes, Latin/Cyrillic homoglyphs, `12х45`, `c:62`, `a7iv → a7 iv`, `4070ti → 4070 ti`, `15r3 → 15 r3`; hyphen codes like `XDJ-RX3`, `67-01`, `BRN-F56` stay whole; key joins tokens with `-` so `DDJ-FLX4` = `DDJ FLX4`) → brand registry (~150 brands seen in CORE/WATCH/QUERY or named in scope; aliases, series, `=complete` series, implied series like `iphone`/`magicbook`/`steam deck`, per-brand token synonyms; unambiguous code prefixes DDJ/XDJ/CDJ/DJM → Pioneer, Osmo → DJI; GPU chips only in the PC domain) → model = tokens right after the brand: series, codes with digits, roman numerals, Pro/Max/Ti/…; stops at the first non-model token, at variants (storage, battery, power, colour, year, frame size) and at codes after a modifier (`Mini 4 Pro RC2`). Up to 3 noise words between brand and model are skipped only if a code/series follows. Domain rules by category: laptop CPU/GPU are specs and the machine type (16IRX9, BRN-F56) is part of the key; phones — model before the comma; numbers alone are a model only for phone/laptop/pc; fashion — only known model lines (brand alone is never a product).
- Confidence: HIGH brand + model with a code; MEDIUM complete series without code or numbers-only model; LOW brand only; UNKNOWN no brand. Only HIGH/MEDIUM get a `canonical_key` (`pioneer|ddj-flx4`) and a `display_name` (`Pioneer DDJ-FLX4`).
- Storage: `products` (canonical_key UNIQUE, brand, model, display_name, best confidence, first/last seen, extractor_version, created_from); current identity on `ads` (`product_id` for HIGH/MEDIUM only, `identity_conf`, `identity_brand`, `identity_variant`, `extractor_version`) — chosen over a separate ad↔product table: one current product per listing, cheap joins; history in `product_assignments` (one row per change of an ad's product). Listings get their product when recorded from search. `ads.model_key` stays as legacy/debug; `/report`'s model groups and the radar still use it (to be replaced when Product Hunter output lands).
- Clusters count distinct `external_id` per product; query sightings never add listings.
- Offline: `python -m scripts.rebuild_products [--dry-run]` re-identifies all stored titles, logs changes, prunes products without listings, prints diagnostics (confidence shares, products, median listings/product, singletons, top clusters, brand-only titles, suspicious clusters: price p75/p25 > 3 or ≥ 3 differing digit codes across titles). A test asserts the module imports neither Playwright nor the browser provider.
- goofish links use «brand model» when the product is recognised; old token fallback otherwise.
- Tests: 170-title tracked corpus (`tests/fixtures/product_titles.json`, 108 real titles from the DB + 62 composed from real model formats for DJ/photo/GPU/Lenovo/fashion/consoles/auto, which are not in the DB yet), same/different-model pairs, persistence, rebuild, diagnostics.
- Live DB 2026-10-04 (backup `data/app.db.bak-20261004-phase2`): 2 797 ads; CORE+WATCH+QUERY 1 095 ads → 219 products; HIGH 45.2%, MEDIUM 3.1%, LOW 15.0%, UNKNOWN 36.7% (mostly titles with no brand: «Электровелосипед», «Велосипед взрослый»).

## ADR-023: Product Candidate Engine — offline, search-page signals only (Product Hunter, phase 3) (2026-10-04)
- Status: accepted (user command «Phase 3»). A candidate means «worth a card load in Phase 4», not «proven demand». Zero Avito requests: `scripts/product_candidates` imports neither Playwright nor the browser (test).
- Pre-flight (commit d22d97b): product first/last seen = MIN/MAX of its listings (touched on every sighting, restored by rebuild); a query-only listing is re-identified with the real category's domain when it shows up there; diagnostics use the stored key and each listing's domain; ambiguous brand names (Trek, Giant, Focus, Look, Cube, Factor, Felt, Scott, Canyon, Polygon, Specialized, KTM, Merida, ADO, Acne, Essentials/FOG, Represent, Supreme, NB, LV, Pulsar, Sigma) match only in their domains; `domain_of` gained photo/music/action/console/auto/stroller/hifi. Real case: «Pro Trek Casio» strap was matched as Trek.
- Eligible listings: product identity HIGH/MEDIUM, price ≥ 8 000, at least one of CORE/WATCH/QUERY/EXPLORE (category or query sighting); one row per external_id however many query sightings. Market date: posted_at from card/seller (HIGH) > from search (MEDIUM; day precision, may be a «raise») > bot first_seen (LOW, fallback only).
- Metrics per product: listings 24h/72h/7d/14d, organic vs promoted, live, distinct ads, cities 7d/14d, known shops 7d/14d (not «sellers»: private sellers are anonymous in search), seller-data coverage, top-shop share, distinct days, price p25/median/p75, first/latest market seen with date confidence, age, reliable-date share, recent 3d vs previous 3d, categories, scopes, query sightings, prior coverage (how far back data for the product's categories goes before it first appeared).
- Paths: REPEATED — ≥ 3 organic listings in 14 days. EMERGING — dated (not bot-first-seen), ≤ 5 days on the market, ≥ 2 organic dated listings in 72h (or growth), and the category was already watched ≥ 3 days before the product appeared (otherwise the first broad crawl makes every product «new»: on the live data 26 products were stopped by this). GROWTH (recent 3d ≥ 3, ≥ 2× previous 3d, previous ≥ 1) and FROM_ZERO need ≥ 7 days of history. High price alone never creates a candidate; HIGH_TICKET (median ≥ category p75) only strengthens one. ATTENTION is reserved for Phase 4.
- Independence: LOW if one shop has ≥ 70% of listings with ≥ 50% seller coverage (checked first) or one city + one day + ≤ 1 shop; HIGH if ≥ 3 cities or ≥ 3 shops or ≥ 2 cities over ≥ 3 days; else MEDIUM. Candidate confidence (separate from identity confidence): REPEATED → independence (capped at MEDIUM below 5 organic listings); EMERGING-only → MEDIUM with ≥ 2 cities else LOW; −1 for MEDIUM identity, −1 if < 50% of dates are reliable, at most MEDIUM while history < 2 days. Every step is written to `why`. Order is deterministic (both paths > REPEATED > EMERGING, then confidence, independence, organic count, recency, price) — scope is not a bonus.
- Storage: `product_candidates` (product_id PK, status NEW/ACTIVE/COOLED, first/last candidate at, confidence, reason codes, last evaluated, last metrics snapshot) and `candidate_log` (product × day). checking/listed/rejected come with Telegram feedback; nothing in the schema blocks them.
- Live run 2026-10-04 (history 1.0 day): 661 eligible listings, 283 products, 59 candidates — all REPEATED (EMERGING/GROWTH not yet provable); confidence MEDIUM 42 / LOW 17; scopes CORE 26 / WATCH 17 / EXPLORE 16 / QUERY 0. Rejected: 189 single listing, 26 insufficient prior coverage, 7 two listings not new, 2 mostly promoted. Saved as NEW.
